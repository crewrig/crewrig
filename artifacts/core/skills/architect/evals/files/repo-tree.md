# Repository facts

The CLI `crewctl` has a flag `--target` used by:

- `artifacts/core/skills/deploy/scripts/push.ts` (calls `crewctl --target`)
- `artifacts/core/skills/deploy/SKILL.md` (documents the flag)
- `docs/cli-reference.md` (documents the flag)
- the CI workflow `.github/workflows/release.yml` (calls `crewctl --target prod`)
- two external repositories, `acme/infra` and `acme/dashboards`, that call `crewctl --target`

Built copies of `artifacts/core/skills/deploy/` are committed under `.claude/skills/deploy/`,
`.gemini/skills/deploy/`, `.github/skills/deploy/` and `.agents/skills/deploy/`; they are
regenerated by `node scripts/build-components.ts` and a CI job fails when they drift from the
source.
