# Staged reusable workflows for the GitHub CI

Delta-01 removes the ruleset change that spec 0287 R5 required. The rulesets `main-protected` and `release-protected` require the status contexts `ratchet` and `lint-typescript`; a called workflow reports its jobs as `<caller job id> / <job>`, so hosting those two jobs in `_ci-gate.yml` would rename the contexts to `gate / ratchet` and `gate / lint-typescript` and force a ruleset update that the implementing account cannot perform (it holds the maintain role, not admin) and that the owner must not be needed for. This delta therefore defines `ratchet` and `lint-typescript` directly in `.github/workflows/build.yml` as ordinary top-level jobs, so the required contexts are unchanged. They remain capabilities of group `gate` in `ci/ci-capabilities.yml`. The version is bumped to a major because requirements R1 to R5, R9, R11, R13, R14 and R20 change in a way that invalidates an implementation written against version 1.0.0; no implementation has merged yet. Every requirement not named below is unchanged.

## ADDED

- **R21 (hosting exception for the required contexts).** Exactly two jobs, `ratchet` and `lint-typescript`, are exempt from the rule that a capability's job lives in its group's callee file. They SHALL be defined in `.github/workflows/build.yml` as top-level jobs with steps (using the `ci-job` action, with no `uses:` of a callee), under their unchanged job keys, so they report as the contexts `ratchet` and `lint-typescript`. Rationale: required status check names are a branch-protection contract, and a workflow restructuring must not rename them. No ruleset, classic branch protection or `gh api` call is changed by this spec or its implementation. Acceptance: the wiring test of R9 asserts that both keys are top-level jobs of `build.yml`, have steps, and appear in no `_ci-*.yml` file.
