# Split checker self-tests from checks

## Intent

Today the `command:` of many fast-tier capabilities in `ci/ci-capabilities.yml` bundles a check with the regression suite of the checker that performs it. For example `lint-specs` runs the `scripts/tests/spec-linter-*.test.ts` suites and then `task spec:lint`. The self-tests therefore run on every change that matches the check's `paths:`, even when the checker itself is untouched. They account for about 47 s of the 60 s `ci:pre-push` budget (spec 0279, `docs/pre-push-guard.md`) and push the hook to 110-135 s under load. This sub-spec, slice 1 of spec 0280 (R2, issue #1778, WP-C of epic #1761), splits each bundling capability into a check capability and a checker-test capability, so the self-tests run only when the checker's own sources or tests change. It has no predecessor; slice 2 (issue #1779) follows it (spec 0280 R7).

## Requirements

1. **R1 (inventory).** The slice SHALL record, in the PR description and in `docs/pre-push-guard.md`, the inventory of fast-tier capabilities (`ci/pre-push-tier.json`, spec 0279 R9 and delta-01) whose `command:` bundles a check with its checker's own self-tests. The candidates, taken from `taskfiles/ci.yml`, are: `check-taskfile-tree`, `check-taskfile-commands`, `lint-specs`, `lint-typescript`, `ratchet`, `host-safety`, `path-ownership`, `docs-index`, `cli-matrix-fresh`, `component-drift`, `test-wiring` and `frontmatter`. A capability whose only `--test` invocation IS the check (for example `markdown-links`) is not a bundle and SHALL stay as is, each exclusion stated with its reason. Capabilities outside the fast tier that bundle self-tests (for example `build-components-ts`) are out of scope for this slice unless the inventory shows one in the Exhaustive Run's critical path; the inventory lists them as follow-up. Acceptance: the inventory names every candidate as split or excluded with a reason, and no fast-tier capability is missing from it.

2. **R2 (split rule and naming).** Each bundling capability `<id>` SHALL become two capabilities: `<id>`, which keeps its id, trigger, `requires:` and the data `paths:` it checks, with a `command:` that runs the check only; and `<id>-selftest`, which runs the checker's own suites. The suffix `-selftest` is decided because it keeps the check's id stable (spec 0280 R9 requires stable task names, and the tier file, GitHub job key and GitLab job name of the check do not change), sorts next to its check, and names what the capability is. Each `<id>-selftest` SHALL have its own `paths:` per trigger, limited to: the checker's own script and library sources, the self-test files it runs, the fixtures they read, the `ci/ci-capabilities.yml` entries or Taskfile files whose shape the checker validates when the self-test reads them, and the capability's own workflow, Taskfile and reference lines. The check capability SHALL keep the data paths it checks, and SHALL also list the checker's sources so that a changed checker still runs the check. Acceptance: for each split pair, a changeset touching only the data paths selects `<id>` and not `<id>-selftest`; a changeset touching only the checker's tests selects `<id>-selftest`; a changeset touching the checker's source selects both.

3. **R3 (tasks, tier and admission).** `taskfiles/ci.yml` SHALL gain one `ci:<id>-selftest` task per new capability, hold the self-test commands there only (never in the root `Taskfile.yml`), and drop them from `ci:<id>`. The fast tier file `ci/pre-push-tier.json` SHALL list `<id>` with its re-measured time, and SHALL list `<id>-selftest` only if it passes the admission rule (spec 0279: deterministic, no container, network or secret, at most 30 s alone); a self-test capability that is admitted runs in `ci:pre-push` only when its own `paths:` match. A self-test suite that exceeds the 30 s cap SHALL be left out of the tier and run in CI and the Exhaustive Run only. The parity guard `scripts/check-ci-pre-push-parity.ts` SHALL stay green without being weakened: each `ci:<id>` and `ci:<id>-selftest` task matches the commands of its capability, and each tier entry has both. Acceptance: `scripts/check-ci-pre-push-parity.ts` exits zero on the repository and exits non-zero on a fixture where a `-selftest` capability command differs from its task.

4. **R4 (engines and generated files).** `.gitlab-ci.yml` SHALL be regenerated by `node scripts/build-ci.ts`, and `check-ci-parity` (`scripts/check-ci-parity.ts`) SHALL stay green. In `.github/workflows/build.yml` each split job SHALL be split into two jobs whose keys equal the capability ids `<id>` and `<id>-selftest` (ADR-0012 traceability), each with the `paths:` of its capability, so a `-selftest` job is skipped on a change outside its paths exactly as the check job is. Every new test file added by the slice SHALL be wired in CI (the `test-wiring` capability stays green). Acceptance: `check-ci-parity` and `node scripts/build-ci.ts --check` (or the repository's equivalent drift check) pass; every job key in `build.yml` equals a capability id.

5. **R5 (nothing is lost).** The self-test capabilities SHALL still run in CI on every change that matches their `paths:`, and in the Exhaustive Run on every run regardless of paths (spec 0147 and its delta-02). The Exhaustive Run's tool provisioning guard SHALL cover the new capabilities. Acceptance: a test or recorded run shows the Exhaustive Run's capability list contains every `-selftest` id, and that no command line of the former bundles was dropped: the union of the commands of `<id>` and `<id>-selftest` equals the former `command:` of `<id>`, apart from the `npm install` setup each job still needs.

6. **R6 (per-slice invariants, spec 0280 R9).** The slice SHALL, at its merge: keep CI green; be reversible by reverting its PRs; leave `.gitlab-ci.yml` regenerated and `check-ci-parity` green; keep `cancel-on-failure`, `changeset-coverage` and `path-ownership` working, including the `required` status checks that name jobs; keep every existing task name stable (only additions); add tasks only to `taskfiles/ci.yml`; and introduce no new framework and no new YAML parser, reusing `scripts/lib/taskfile-tree.ts`, `scripts/lib/ci-parity/reference-read.ts` and `scripts/lib/glob-engine.ts`. Acceptance: the PR shows each invariant green, and a guard-by-guard check of `cancel-on-failure`, `changeset-coverage` and `path-ownership` is listed in the PR.

7. **R7 (measurement protocol and target).** The slice SHALL re-measure `ci:pre-push` and record the figures in `docs/pre-push-guard.md`, replacing the stale table, using the same shape as the existing one so the numbers are comparable: one row per condition with tasks run, wall-clock and CPU time. Protocol: (a) the changeset is a fixed branch of 16 changed files that touches data paths of the split checks but no checker sources, plus one second run whose changeset touches one checker, to show the selected self-test runs only then; (b) conditions are idle (no other heavy process), light contention (load average about 10 on 10 cores) and heavy contention (load average 47 to 60 produced by the owner's usual concurrent agents; when that load cannot be reproduced, a synthetic load of CPU-bound `node` processes, at least `cores x 2` in number, is used and stated); (c) the machine's core count and OS are recorded; (d) the median of at least 3 runs is reported per condition; (e) CPU time comes from `time` on the whole run. The `node:24` Linux container row is re-measured when the container is available, otherwise marked not re-measured. Acceptance: the target is `ci:pre-push` within 60 s under the owner's usual multi-agent load; when it is not, the doc states the shortfall and its cause (for example the capabilities that remain over the cap), and the stale "planned work" sentence about the split is removed.

8. **R8 (line-count report, spec 0280 R10).** The PR SHALL report the YAML line counts of `.github/workflows/build.yml`, `ci/ci-capabilities.yml` and `.gitlab-ci.yml` after the slice, against the baseline of spec 0280 R10 (3,758, 5,189, 3,734; total 12,681), with the delta per surface. This slice is expected to grow the total, because the split adds capabilities; the report SHALL state the growth and that slices 3 and 5 are where the reduction lands. The sub-spec asserts no line-count target for this slice. Acceptance: the PR carries the table and the total.

## Scenarios

**Scenario:** A data-only change skips the self-tests

Given a changeset that edits only a spec file under `specs/`
When `task ci:pre-push` runs
Then `lint-specs` runs, `lint-specs-selftest` does not run, and the run is well under the 60 s budget.

**Scenario:** A checker change runs its self-tests

Given a changeset that edits `scripts/lib/spec-linter/` (or the spec linter's test files)
When `task ci:pre-push` runs
Then `lint-specs-selftest` is selected, when admitted to the fast tier, together with `lint-specs`.

**Scenario:** Self-tests still guard the checker in CI and the Exhaustive Run

Given the Exhaustive Run
When it runs on schedule
Then every `<id>-selftest` capability runs regardless of paths and its failure turns the run red.

**Scenario:** The parity guard catches drift between a self-test task and its capability

Given a `-selftest` capability whose `command:` differs from `task ci:<id>-selftest`'s command list
When `scripts/check-ci-pre-push-parity.ts` runs
Then it exits non-zero and names the capability.

**Scenario:** The target is missed with a stated cause

Given the heavy-contention measurement of `ci:pre-push` exceeds 60 s
When the documentation is updated
Then `docs/pre-push-guard.md` shows the figure, names the capabilities that still dominate, and does not claim the budget is met.

## Out of scope

- Modifying `.githooks/pre-push` or `scripts/ci-pre-push.ts`: a separate hotfix, issue #1783, owns them. This slice SHALL NOT touch either file; if the split needs a wrapper change, it is raised on that issue.
- Splitting capabilities outside the fast tier, beyond listing them as follow-up in the inventory.
- Changing what any check verifies, changing triggers, or changing `paths:` semantics other than the split.
- Making `command:` equal `task ci:<id>` (slice 3, issue #1780) and the uniform Task provisioning (slice 2, issue #1779).
- Reducing the line counts of the CI surfaces (slices 3 and 5).

## Open questions

- Which of the twelve candidates pass the 30 s admission cap as `-selftest` capabilities? `lint-specs`, `path-ownership` and `frontmatter` took 23 s to 27 s with their self-tests bundled, so the cap may leave some self-tests out of the fast tier. To settle by measurement in PLAN.
- Is the `-selftest` suffix acceptable to the `capability-id` format and the GitHub job-key rules of `scripts/lib/ci-parity/reference-read.ts`? To confirm in PLAN; the alternative is a prefix `selftest-<id>`.
- Can the heavy-contention row be reproduced on demand, or only observed when other agents happen to run? The protocol allows a synthetic load; the owner may prefer to accept observed runs only.
